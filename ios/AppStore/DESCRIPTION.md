### Description

Small blocks. Big moments.

Find your flow, clear the next line, and turn a quiet solo session into a friendly rivalry. pt.TetrisDuel brings classic falling-block play to iPhone and iPad with colorful, lit 3D cubes and three ways to play.

YOUR GAME, YOUR WAY
• Solo: build your score, sharpen your placement, and keep up as the game gets faster.
• Two players on one iPad: play side by side, with a separate board and independent touch controls for each player.
• Nearby duels: host or join a game on two iPhones or iPads over local or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. No account or internet connection is required for play.

SAME PIECES. YOUR OWN PACE.
In a duel, both players receive the same piece sequence. Clear multiple lines to send garbage rows to your opponent, cancel incoming attacks with your own clears, and stay in the game longer than your rival. Combos, consecutive four-line clears, and perfect clears reward smart play. Ready for another round? Request a rematch and keep the rivalry going.

SEE YOUR NEXT MOVE
Keep a piece in Hold, preview the next three pieces, and use the landing outline to plan your placement. Rotate in either direction, move across the board, soft drop, or drop a piece straight into place.

BUILT FOR YOUR SESSION
• A classic 10 × 20 playing field with a fresh 3D look
• Score, line count, and level tracking
• Increasing speed every ten cleared lines
• Touch controls and external keyboard support
• Pause and resume
• Switchable sound and haptic feedback
• English and Russian interfaces

Nearby play requires Wi-Fi and Local Network permission. Use the same app version on both devices and keep both apps open during the match. Shared-device two-player mode is available on iPad.
