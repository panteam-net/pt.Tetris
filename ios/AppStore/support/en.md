## Contact support

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, contact the app's maintainers through
[the pt.TetrisDuel support tracker](https://github.com/panteam-net/pt.Tetris/issues).
You can [open a support request](https://github.com/panteam-net/pt.Tetris/issues/new)
after signing in to a GitHub account.

Please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, game mode,
and the steps that reproduce the problem. Describe what you expected and what
happened. You can write in English or Russian.

Support requests on GitHub are public. Include only the technical information
needed to describe the problem.

## Nearby multiplayer does not connect

1. Use the same app version on both devices and turn on Wi-Fi.
2. Allow the app in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network**.
3. Choose **Host a nearby game** on one device and **Join a nearby game** on the other.
4. Select the host's name and accept the invitation on the host device.
5. Keep both apps open in the foreground during the match.

If discovery fails or a connection is lost, return to the menu and create a new
room. Nearby multiplayer uses local or peer-to-peer connectivity and does not
require a game account.

## Two players on one device

Shared-device multiplayer is available on iPad. Choose **Two players · this
iPad** from the menu. Each player has a separate board and independent controls.
On iPhone, use nearby multiplayer with a second device to play a duel.

## Controls, pause, and sound

- Hold **Left**, **Right**, or **Down** to repeat a move.
- Rotation, **Hold**, and **Drop** act once per press.
- Use the pause button to pause or resume the game.
- Use the speaker button to toggle sound and haptic feedback.
- External keyboards are supported; **P** or **Esc** pauses the game.
- Open **How to play** using the question-mark button during a game.
